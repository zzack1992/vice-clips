Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how Vice Clips and its internal publishing tool, the Vice Clips Bot (together, "Vice Clips", "we"), handle information. Vice Clips is operated by Zack Zulkiewicz.

YouTube API Services

The Vice Clips Bot uses YouTube API Services to upload, schedule and read statistics for videos on the Vice Clips YouTube channel. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access and why

  • Upload access to the Vice Clips channel — to publish and schedule our own videos.
  • Read-only channel and video data (titles, view counts, likes, comments counts) and YouTube Analytics (views, average view duration/percentage) for our own videos — to measure which edits perform best and improve future videos.

The bot only signs in to the channel owned by its operator. It does not access, collect or store data about other YouTube users, viewers or channels, and it does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Storage and retention

The Google sign-in token and video statistics are stored only on the operator's own computer. Statistics are kept while the channel is active and deleted on request. Downloaded source clips are deleted automatically within 7 days.

Revoking access

Access granted to the Vice Clips Bot can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

Visitors and viewers

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers and collects no personal information. Viewing our videos on YouTube, TikTok or Instagram is governed by those platforms' own privacy policies.

Streamers

Clips feature publicly broadcast Twitch content and are credited to the original streamer. To request removal of a clip, email us and we will remove it within 24 hours.
